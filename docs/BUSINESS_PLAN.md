# Business Plan & Commercial Viability: Proactive AI Desktop Pet

> Hackathon Evaluation Focus: **Commercial Potential (35% Weighting)**  
> Reference: Slides 03, 18, 19 of Stage I Proposal

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Workers and students face unprecedented information fragmentation: actionable commitments are scattered across emails, chat threads, and ad-hoc calendar entries. Cloud-based AI solutions demand high ongoing subscription fees, raise severe privacy concerns over corporate/personal data, and consume excessive battery through continuous polling.

**Proactive AI Desktop Pet** delivers an on-device, privacy-preserving desktop assistant powered by edge acceleration (ASUS UGen300). By operating locally and activating only when new data arrives, it reduces daily routine cognitive load while keeping all personal data strictly within the user's computer.

## 4. Monetization & Tiering Strategy (Slide 19)

We adopt a **Product-Led Growth (PLG)** model with a functional free core:

```
[ Discover ] ---> [ Activate ] ---> [ Useful Reminder ] ---> [ Retain ] ---> [ Pay ]
```

1. **Free Core Tier**:
   - Local AI pet widget with basic calendar and email integration.
   - Core on-device INT8 parsing and proactive notifications.
   - Complete local offline privacy.
2. **Personal Premium Tier ($3.99/month)**:
   - Multi-source connectors (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, WhatsApp Desktop).
   - Advanced dynamic strategy tuning (custom travel buffers, bespoke quiet hours).
   - Multi-pet personality engine and customized voice/speech styles.
3. **Cosmetics & IP Collaborations (Voluntary Add-on)**:
   - Pixel art, 3D mecha, and licensed anime companion skins.
4. **ASUS Ecosystem & Hardware Bundling (B2B / OEM)**:
   - Pre-installed as the signature AI companion on ASUS AI PCs equipped with the UGen300 accelerator.

# Business hypotheses
Primary audience: freelancers and project workers whose commitments arrive across tools.
Secondary audience: university students.
Value proposition: reduce manual event entry and forgotten preparation while keeping interpretation local.

The MVP validates reminder usefulness, extraction correctness, correction frequency,
notification burden, restart reliability and local runtime cost.
Pilot interviews and retention data are required before claiming demand.
Potential pricing: a free core, a personal premium tier for additional integrations,
and optional character cosmetics. No price or conversion rate has been validated.

Competition rubric from the supplied school deck:
innovation 30%, commercial potential 35%, practical value 25%, storytelling 10%.
Demonstrate one trustworthy workflow and cite measured evidence.
Do not claim preinstallation agreements, partnerships or achieved sales.
